
# Kechen Motor

### Micro Motors and Precision Motion Solutions for OEM Applications

## About Us

Shenzhen Kechen Micro Motor Technology Co., Ltd. is a micro motor manufacturer established in March 2017 in Shenzhen, China.

We specialize in compact electric motors and geared drive systems for OEM equipment, precision instruments, and industrial automation. Our product development and manufacturing capabilities support applications requiring controlled motion, compact dimensions, and application-specific electrical performance.

## Motor Technologies

Our product range includes:

- **Brushed DC Motors** — Compact DC motors for continuous and intermittent drive applications.
- **Brushless DC Motors (BLDC)** — Electronically commutated motors for efficient and reliable operation.
- **Stepper Motors** — Permanent magnet and hybrid stepper motors for controlled positioning.
- **Micro Gear Motors** — Compact motor assemblies with spur, planetary, and worm gear reduction.
- **Linear Stepper Motors** — Lead screw driven motors and miniature linear motion assemblies.
- **Coreless DC Motors** — Low-inertia motors for compact precision drive systems.

## Engineering Applications

Our motors are used in a wide range of motion-control systems, including:

- Medical devices and laboratory instruments
- Industrial automation equipment
- Precision measurement systems
- Compact robotics and automated mechanisms
- Smart home devices and access control systems
- Small pumps, valves, and dispensing mechanisms

## OEM and Customization Support

We support engineering projects with application-specific motor configurations, including:

- Rated voltage and operating speed
- Torque and gear reduction ratios
- Output shaft dimensions and configurations
- Lead screw and linear motion options
- Wiring, connectors, and mounting arrangements
- Motor selection and sample evaluation

Available specifications and customization options depend on the selected motor model and application requirements.
